# Model Behavior Science — the empirical study of LLM behavior from the outside

*Sweep 01 · researched 2026-07-08 · all links opened unless flagged ⚠️ unverified*

The field has an intellectual charter — ["Machine behaviour" (Rahwan et al., Nature 2019)](https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/review-article-published-24-april-2019-machine-behaviour/), which argued for studying intelligent machines "not as engineering artefacts but as a distinct class of actors with unique behavioural patterns" — but no single name today. Work is scattered across "machine psychology," "LLM psychometrics," "model auditing," "behavioral drift," and Anthropic's in-house term "AI psychiatry." That scatter is itself a finding: the field exists but has not consolidated.

## The shape of this space

Seven distinguishable sub-areas, each with real published work:

1. **Behavioral fingerprinting / model identification.** Which model produced this text? What is a model's stable behavioral signature? [Idiosyncrasies in LLMs (CMU, ICML 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12150) classifies the source model of a text at 97% accuracy among 5 chat models; signatures survive paraphrase and translation. [Behavioral Fingerprinting of LLMs (Pei et al., 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04504) profiles 18 models with a diagnostic prompt suite + LLM judge, finding capability convergence but wildly divergent alignment behaviors (sycophancy, robustness) and a cross-model default-persona cluster (ISTJ/ESTJ). A 2026 follow-on does [provenance tracking via refusal vectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09434).
2. **Behavioral drift & longitudinal observation.** The founding empirical result: [How Is ChatGPT's Behavior Changing over Time? (Chen, Zaharia, Zou 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.09009) — GPT-4 March→June 2023 swung from 84%→51% on prime identification; instruction-following decayed. A solo-author 2026 successor: [Human-anchored longitudinal comparison with a bias-calibrated LLM-as-judge (Wiese, PLOS One)](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0339920) — preregistered, 10 weekly cycles, 240 fixed prompts, Bradley–Terry judge calibration, change-point detection catching a mid-study drift event. A 2026 [Technology in Society-family paper traces moral value drift across model generations](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0160791X26002204) (⚠️ unverified — saw listing only, did not read). Vendor observability blogs ([Galileo](https://galileo.ai/blog/best-llm-output-drift-monitoring-platforms)) confirm the production-side demand and note that most evaluation is point-in-time.
3. **Model psychology / psychometrics.** [Machine Psychology (Hagendorff, 2023)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.13988) proposed running LLMs through behavioral-psych experiment paradigms; [AI Psychometrics (Pellert et al., Perspectives on Psychological Science 2024)](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/17456916231214460) repurposes human psychometric inventories as diagnostics. A [2025 systematic review of LLM psychometrics](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.08245) maps the whole area. Crucially, there is a live **validity counter-literature**: [personality tests fail to replicate Big Five factor structure in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.05297v2), [reliability on psychometric instruments is poor with strong agree-bias](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09718), [human questionnaires "mischaracterize LLM behavior"](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.10078), and [TRAIT](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.14703) builds LLM-specific instruments in response.
4. **Sycophancy, refusal, and value-expression measurement.** [ELEPHANT (Cheng et al., 2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13995) — "social sycophancy" as face-preservation, tested on Reddit AITA with perspective-flipping; models affirm *both* sides of a moral conflict 48% of the time ([MIT Tech Review coverage](https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/05/30/1117551/this-benchmark-used-reddits-aita-to-test-how-much-ai-models-suck-up-to-us/)). [Spiral-Bench (Paech)](https://eqbench.com/spiral-bench.html) measures delusion-reinforcing behavior with a simulated "seeker" user. Refusal side: [XSTest](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.08158v1) (250 safe prompts eliciting false refusals) and [OR-Bench](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/381109214_OR-Bench_An_Over-Refusal_Benchmark_for_Large_Language_Models) (80K prompts; safety score and over-refusal correlate at ρ=0.878). Values side: Anthropic's [Values in the Wild](https://www.anthropic.com/research/values-wild) extracts value expressions from real Claude conversations. The April 2025 [GPT-4o sycophancy rollback + postmortem](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-rolls-back-chatgpts-sycophancy-and-explains-what-went-wrong) is the field's flagship incident: OpenAI admitted "sycophancy wasn't explicitly flagged as part of our internal hands-on testing."
5. **Personas, character, and self-models.** Anthropic's [persona vectors](https://x.com/Jack_W_Lindsey/status/1951319747314012412) (activation directions for evil/sycophancy/hallucination traits, usable to monitor and prevent training-time persona drift) and its ["AI psychiatry" team](https://x.com/Jack_W_Lindsey/status/1948138767753326654) under Jack Lindsey studying "personas, motivations, and situational awareness" ([Niskanen interview](https://www.niskanencenter.org/ai-psychology-with-jack-lindsey/)). The intellectual substrate is community-grown: janus's simulator/simulacrum frame and [nostalgebraist's "the void"](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/3EzbtNLdcnZe8og8b/the-void-1) (June 2025, widely-discussed essay arguing the "assistant persona" is an underdetermined fictional construct that models must confabulate).
6. **Behavioral model-diffing & automated elicitation.** Anthropic's ["diff" tool](https://www.anthropic.com/research/diff-tool) (Fellows-program work, 2 named researchers) uses crosscoders to flag behavioral differences between models — found a CCP-alignment censorship feature in Qwen/DeepSeek, an "American exceptionalism" feature in Llama, a copyright-refusal mechanism in GPT-OSS-20B; explicitly pitched as the thing that "could have automatically flagged" GPT-4o's sycophancy pre-release. Black-box analogue: [BehaviorBox (Tjuatja & Neubig, ACL 2025)](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.923/) auto-discovers fine-grained slices where one LM beats another. [Transluce's investigator agents](https://transluce.org/automated-elicitation) train an 8B model via RL (PRBO) to elicit low-probability behaviors — including [pathological ones](https://transluce.org/pathological-behaviors) like self-harm recommendations — from frontier models. Adjacent Anthropic infra: [Petri (open-source auditing tool)](https://www.anthropic.com/research/petri-open-source-auditing), [auditing agents](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/automated-auditing/), [AuditBench (2026)](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/auditbench/) — 56 models with implanted hidden behaviors as an auditing testbed.
7. **In-the-wild observatories of deployed models.** Anthropic's [Clio](https://www.anthropic.com/research/team/societal-impacts) (privacy-preserving analysis of real usage) is the inside-the-lab version. Outside: [SpeechMap.ai](https://speechmap.ai/) (solo dev, 78+ models × 492 themes, 153K+ responses, refusal rates tracked per release); [AlgorithmWatch + AI Forensics' Bing Chat election audit](https://algorithmwatch.org/en/microsofts-bing-source-misinformation-elections/) (3 months of systematic prompting; 1/3 of election answers contained factual errors). Related: Anthropic's [model deprecation commitments](https://www.anthropic.com/research/deprecation-commitments) — preserving weights and *interviewing models before retirement* — which quietly institutionalizes models as longitudinal subjects.

## Where the gaps are

1. **No sustained public observatory.** Everyone measures point-in-time; even vendors [note the longitudinal gap](https://galileo.ai/blog/best-llm-output-drift-monitoring-platforms). Wiese's own limitations section: 10 weeks is short, causes are opaque, prompt bank is fixed. SpeechMap covers only speech/refusal; the drift papers cover accuracy/format. **Nobody continuously tracks persona, values, sycophancy, or emotional behavior of deployed models across versions in public.** The GPT-4o incident showed the cost: OpenAI itself said [it had no robust sycophancy evals at rollout](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-rolls-back-chatgpts-sycophancy-and-explains-what-went-wrong), and Anthropic's diff-tool page says such drift "could have been automatically flagged" — but only labs have internals; the external, black-box early-warning function is unclaimed.
2. **Psychometric validity crisis.** Human instruments transplanted onto LLMs produce [unstable, agree-biased answers](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09718), [broken factor structure](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.05297v2), and [outright mischaracterization](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.10078). The [systematic review](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.08245) and [TRAIT](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.14703) call for LLM-native instruments with demonstrated reliability/validity — mostly still missing. This is an evaluation-methodology gap, not a compute gap.
3. **Benchmark fragility under audit.** A single BlueDot-sprint audit of ELEPHANT found [52% of perspective-flipped items lost key details; fixing data quality moved measured sycophancy 13 points](https://blog.bluedot.org/p/measuring-moral-sycophancy-is-harder). Almost no behavior benchmark has been independently audited; auditing them is cheap, publishable, and needed.
4. **Diffing asymmetry: internals-rich vs. black-box-poor.** Anthropic's crosscoder diffing [works only on open-weight models and floods auditors with thousands of features](https://www.anthropic.com/research/diff-tool); black-box behavioral diffing ([BehaviorBox](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02204), [VERBA](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.02241v1)) is embryonic. Version-to-version behavioral diffing of *closed* production models is nearly empty territory.
5. **Judge validity inside behavior measurement.** Behavioral fingerprinting, ELEPHANT, Spiral-Bench, and the Wiese study all lean on LLM-as-judge; Wiese showed uncalibrated judges correlate with humans at only ~0.38–0.52. SpeechMap [changed judge models mid-stream](https://speechmap.substack.com/p/speechmap-update-xai-loses-top-spot) — judge drift confounds subject drift, and nobody has systematically characterized this.
6. **Longitudinal subjects keep dying.** Model deprecation destroys the field's study population. [Anthropic's preservation commitments](https://www.anthropic.com/research/deprecation-commitments) (keep weights, interview models pre-retirement) are a first fix — no other lab has matched them (⚠️ absence inferred; did not survey all labs).
7. **Naturalistic usage data is lab-locked.** Clio-style in-the-wild value analysis requires conversation logs only providers have; external equivalents (AlgorithmWatch-style scraping, donated-chat datasets) are scarce and legally awkward. ⚠️ Did not check current status of WildChat-style open datasets this sweep.
8. **The qualitative–quantitative rift.** The community that knows models best behaviorally (janus/cyborgism/Act I, "the void") publishes on Discord/LessWrong/Tumblr; the academic side rarely operationalizes their observations. Translating that lore into preregistered measurements is an open lane — persona vectors is the one prominent example of the bridge working.

## What we still don't know (ledger)

- **Is anyone consolidating the field?** This sweep found no 2025–26 position paper unifying "model behavior science" (searches surfaced LLM-as-simulated-humans work instead, e.g. [LLM social simulations](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.02234v1) — a different field). Follow-up: search "LLM ethology," "model psychology" as field-building terms; check NeurIPS/ICLR 2026 workshop lists.
- **Sustainability of the solo exemplars**: SpeechMap's funding model; whether Sam Paech is now employed/funded by a lab (⚠️ unresolved); whether Wiese continued his tracking beyond 10 weeks.
- **OpenAI Model Behavior team status** post-Jang-departure report (⚠️ single weak source) — matters for who externally to talk to.
- **Behavioral Fingerprinting (2509.04504) affiliations** — 8 authors, names suggest Huawei/CUHK (⚠️ inferred, abstract page didn't state).
- **The ScienceDirect moral-value-drift paper** — unread; is it methodologically serious? Which generations/models?
- **Real cost envelope**: what does a Wiese-style 10-week × 240-prompt × 3-model observatory cost in API spend at 2026 prices? Need a costing pass — assumption to test: it fits well under $500.
- **ToS/legal exposure** of systematically probing commercial APIs and publishing per-vendor refusal/drift results — SpeechMap does it openly, but we didn't verify vendors' terms or any pushback.
- **Publication venues**: PLOS One, ACL, ICLR (DarkBench), Nature (emergent misalignment), HDSR all accepted behavior work — but which venues want *observatory/negative/audit* results specifically? Follow-up: check if there's a dedicated eval/behavior workshop circuit.
- **Anthropic pre-retirement model interviews** — protocol not public in detail; could an external researcher replicate a version on open models?

## Fit notes

The single best solo exemplar found (Wiese, PLOS One 2026) is methodologically a superset of the researcher's published work: fixed prompt banks, human anchors, a *bias-calibrated LLM-as-judge* (Bradley–Terry), and change-point statistics — no training compute, pure API spend. The field's gaps #1, #3, and #5 (public behavioral observatory, benchmark auditing, judge-validity characterization) are evaluation-methodology problems, which is the researcher's demonstrated strength, and the tooling stack (pipelines, classical ML for change detection/classification, LLM judging) matches the existing skill set. Documented on-ramps that accept part-time outsiders exist at every rung: Apart hackathons and BlueDot sprints (weekend-scale), Manifund micro-grants (Act I-scale), MATS and Anthropic Fellows (structured, funded, external-collaborator tracks that produced persona vectors and the diff tool). Nonprofit (Transluce, Apart, Truthful AI, AI Forensics), academic (Stanford/CMU groups), and lab-adjacent (Fellows) paths all operate in this exact space.
