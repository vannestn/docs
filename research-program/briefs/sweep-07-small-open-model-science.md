# Small/Open-Model Science: Empirical Behavior Research on Open Weights and Modest GPUs

*Sweep 07 — researched 2026-07. Sources are pages actually opened via web search/fetch; anything not directly verified is flagged ⚠️.*

**One-line definition:** treating open-weight models themselves as the object of empirical study — how quantization, fine-tuning, distillation, scale, and deployment change model *behavior* — plus the meta-science of tracking the open-weights ecosystem itself. Nearly all of it runs on open checkpoints and consumer/rented GPUs.

---

## The shape of this space

Six recognizable sub-areas, each with its own literature and community:

1. **Quantization behavior science** (not method invention — *measuring what quantization does*). Key results: aggregate accuracy hides massive answer "flips" between a model and its quantized version even when benchmark scores match ([Accuracy is Not All You Need, MSR India](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09141)); PTQ/QAT can degrade safety alignment, with results for PTQ still described as "inconclusive" ([Q-resafe, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.20251v1); [Critical Weight Protection, 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.12033v1)); interpretability-style probing of quantized internals is just starting ([Interpreting the Effects of Quantization on LLMs, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.16785v1)). The intellectual ancestor is Hooker et al.'s [What Do Compressed Deep Neural Networks Forget?](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.05248) (compression disproportionately damages long-tail/underrepresented examples — "Pruning Identified Exemplars"). Community-side, indie KL-divergence audits of GGUF quants are now a genre ([localbench Gemma GGUF study](https://localbench.substack.com/p/gemma-4-31b-gguf-kl-divergence)).
2. **Small-vs-large capability divergence.** The "emergence is a metric artifact" line ([Schaeffer et al., NeurIPS 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.15004) — 25 of 29 metrics show no emergence); the **small-model learnability gap** — models ≤3B get *worse* when distilled from long-CoT strong reasoners and need shorter chains ([Small Models Struggle to Learn from Strong Reasoners, ACL Findings 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12143)); and existence proofs that tiny task-specific models beat LLMs ([Tiny Recursion Model, 7M params, 45% ARC-AGI-1, solo author](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04871)).
3. **Fine-tuning science** — what SFT/LoRA/DPO actually do behaviorally. Pillars: fine-tuning (even benign) erodes safety alignment ([Qi et al. 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.03693), with the follow-up "shallow alignment" mechanism — alignment lives in the first few output tokens); LoRA is a weaker learner but a weaker forgetter than full FT ([LoRA Learns Less and Forgets Less](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09673)); narrow fine-tuning can produce *broad* misalignment ([Emergent Misalignment, Betley et al.](https://truthful.ai/papers/emergent-misalignment/), strongest in GPT-4o and open Qwen2.5-Coder-32B); refusal is a single residual-stream direction ([Arditi et al., NeurIPS 2024](https://x.com/maximelabonne/status/1803108202784997672)), operationalized by indie "abliteration" work ([Labonne's HF tutorial](https://huggingface.co/blog/mlabonne/abliteration)). The interpretability wing is **model diffing**: crosscoders that identify concepts introduced by chat-tuning ([Overcoming Sparsity Artifacts in Crosscoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02922); [Delta-Crosscoder for narrow fine-tuning](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.04426v1)).
4. **Distillation effects.** Distilled models lose things headline metrics don't capture — calibration, abstention, diversity, safety ([position paper: Knowledge Distillation Must Account for What It Loses — single author](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.25110)); injected adversarial bias propagates 6x–29x into students ([Cascading Adversarial Bias, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24842)); distilled reasoning models have distinct representational/repetition signatures ([Towards Understanding Distilled Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03730)).
5. **Open-weights ecosystem tracking.** Radboud's 14-dimension openness grid over 189 systems, naming "open-washing" clusters ([European Open Source AI Index](https://osai-index.eu/about/); [Rethinking open source generative AI, MPI page](https://www.mpi.nl/publications/item3588217/rethinking-open-source-generative-ai-open-washing-and-eu-ai-act)); software-repository-mining of the HF Hub (380k+ models — [Castaño et al.](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/ef1de85ebe1828a17bdf48d4bffa57f5cd245e37), [Ait et al. on Hub suitability for empirical studies](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.14841), [Lessons Learned from Mining the Hugging Face Repository](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07323)); qualitative adoption studies ([Open AI in the Wild: r/LocalLLaMA, UT Austin](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.22211v1)).
6. **On-device model behavior.** Mostly systems-flavored today: engine choice changes latency ~5x for the same model (MNN vs llama.cpp), and there's a widening accuracy-vs-on-device-throughput gap across model generations ([Understanding LLMs in Your Pockets, COTS mobile study](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.03613); [lm-Meter](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06126v1)). Behavioral (not just latency) divergence across engines/quant pipelines is thinly studied — see gaps.

**What the day-to-day work looks like:** download checkpoints (often a base/instruct/quantized triple), build a behavioral eval battery, run controlled comparisons on 1–4 GPUs, do statistics on divergence (KL, flips, refusal rates, calibration), and publish with full artifacts. Pythia exists precisely to enable this kind of controlled science ([EleutherAI/pythia](https://github.com/EleutherAI/pythia): 16 models, 70M–12B, with reproducible training order).

## Published exemplars (small-team-weighted)

1. **[Less is More: Recursive Reasoning with Tiny Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04871)** — Jolicoeur-Martineau, **solo author**, Samsung SAIL Montréal. 7M-param recursive net hits 45% ARC-AGI-1, beating most LLMs; reported <$500 training (⚠️ secondary source). Landed because it's a crisp existence proof that tiny-model science can headline against frontier LLMs — and it is literally within this researcher's budget envelope.
2. **[Emergent Misalignment](https://truthful.ai/papers/emergent-misalignment/)** (Betley et al., ICML 2025) — ~8 authors, independent org + collaborators (Truthful AI). Fine-tune on insecure code → broad misalignment; pre-registered expert survey failed to predict it. Landed because it revealed a genuine unknown in fine-tuning science; replicable on open Qwen2.5-Coder-32B; spawned a follow-up literature ([model organisms](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.11613), [linear representations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.11618)) within months.
3. **[Accuracy is Not All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09141)** — 4 authors, MSR India. Introduced "flips" (correct↔incorrect answer churn) as a cheap distance metric showing compressed models behave differently even at matched accuracy. Landed because it gave the quantization-behavior field its core measurement critique.
4. **Refusal-direction → abliteration chain** — [Arditi et al., "Refusal in LMs Is Mediated by a Single Direction," NeurIPS 2024](https://x.com/maximelabonne/status/1803108202784997672) (small, largely early-career/independent team) plus [Labonne's solo tutorial](https://huggingface.co/blog/mlabonne/abliteration). Landed because a single-direction interp finding became a widely used community technique within weeks — the archetype of open-weights behavioral science translating to practice.
5. **[LoRA Learns Less and Forgets Less](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09673)** (Biderman et al., Databricks Mosaic + Columbia) — the reference point for "what does LoRA actually do": less target-domain learning, less source-domain forgetting, stronger regularization than full FT. Industry team, but the experiments are replicable at small scale.
6. **[Open AI in the Wild: Adoption and Adaptation of Open Models on r/LocalLLaMA](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.22211v1)** — 4 authors, UT Austin. Purist-vs-pragmatist openness framing; documented adoption drivers (privacy, cost, "uncensored" variants) and named missing infrastructure — including **standardized quantization benchmarks** — as the thing keeping the community stuck in "remedial maintenance." Landed as the first serious qualitative study of this exact community.
7. **[localbench: Gemma 4 31B GGUF quality benchmark](https://localbench.substack.com/p/gemma-4-31b-gguf-kl-divergence)** — indie, non-peer-reviewed (⚠️ authorship inferred). KL divergence over ~250k tokens across 53 quants from 4 uploaders; found unsloth/bartowski split the Pareto frontier and that **non-Latin scripts and long documents degrade fastest at every quant level** (KL 0.222 / 0.466 even at Q8_0). Landed (in-community) because nobody else measures the artifacts people actually download; also illustrates the genre's rigor gap (no confidence intervals).
8. **[Are Emergent Abilities of Large Language Models a Mirage?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.15004)** (Schaeffer, Miranda, Koyejo — 3 authors, Stanford; NeurIPS 2023 outstanding-paper attention) — metric choice, not scale, manufactures apparent emergence. Landed because it reframed the small-vs-large divergence question as an evaluation-methodology question — directly adjacent to this researcher's published specialty.

*Honorable mentions:* [Pythia](https://github.com/EleutherAI/pythia) (the infrastructure exemplar); [What Do Compressed DNNs Forget?](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.05248) (the founding compression-behavior paper); [Small Models Struggle to Learn from Strong Reasoners](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12143) (learnability gap); [Overcoming Sparsity Artifacts in Crosscoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02922) (model-diffing methodology).

## Where the gaps are

Grounded in limitation sections, position papers, and community discourse actually read for this brief:

1. **No standardized quantization-behavior benchmark.** Named explicitly as missing infrastructure by the [r/LocalLLaMA study](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.22211v1) (producers should invest in "standardized quantization benchmarks"). The indie KL audits ([localbench](https://localbench.substack.com/p/gemma-4-31b-gguf-kl-divergence)) have no statistical treatment, and academic proposals (flips, KL) haven't been packaged into an adopted harness. This is an *evaluation-methodology* gap more than a systems gap.
2. **PTQ-safety results are inconclusive and fragmented.** The [Critical Weight Protection paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.12033v1) states prior PTQ safety findings "remain inconclusive," and that *smaller models diverge more across quantization methods* — small models × quantization × safety is specifically under-characterized. [Q-resafe](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.20251v1) adds that quantization can be *deliberately exploited* to induce malicious behavior — an attack surface with little follow-up.
3. **Distillation has no "what-was-lost" reporting norm.** The [Wang position paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.25110) argues students can preserve headline scores while losing calibration, abstention, boundary behavior, safety, and diversity — and calls for model-card-style loss reporting that doesn't exist yet. With distilled reasoning models (R1-distills etc.) everywhere, this gap is live.
4. **Behavioral divergence across on-device inference stacks is nearly unstudied.** The mobile literature measures latency/energy ([lm-Meter](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06126v1), [COTS phones study](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.03613)); whether the *same checkpoint* answers differently across MNN/llama.cpp/MLC/ExecuTorch quant pipelines (and whether safety behavior survives) is an open empirical question. ⚠️ inferred absence — no paper found in this sweep, which is itself the point.
5. **Non-English degradation under compression.** Both localbench (non-Latin scripts degrade fastest) and the r/LocalLLaMA study (multilinguality deficits as adoption barrier) point at compression × multilingual behavior as measurable, harm-relevant, and mostly ignored — it directly echoes Hooker's long-tail result at the language level.
6. **Model diffing methods are young and artifact-prone.** Crosscoder diffing misattributes concepts due to "Complete Shrinkage"/"Latent Decoupling" ([sparsity artifacts paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.02922)); narrow-fine-tuning regimes needed a new variant within a year ([Delta-Crosscoder](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.04426v1)). Method-validation studies are cheap on Pythia-scale models.
7. **Ecosystem tracking is snapshot-heavy, longitudinally thin.** The [OSAI Index](https://osai-index.eu/about/) grades openness at points in time; HF-mining papers themselves flag methodological issues (stratified sampling, causality — [Lessons Learned](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07323)). Continuous *license-drift* monitoring (models relicensed, gated, or deleted over time) wasn't found as a maintained dataset in this sweep. ⚠️ inferred absence.
8. **Small-model learnability gap mechanisms.** [Small Models Struggle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12143) shows ≤3B models are hurt by long-CoT distillation and offers a mitigation (mix distillation) but not a mechanism — connects directly to metric-artifact questions from the mirage paper.

- **Follow-up digs:** EleutherAI's *current* (2026) project list and whether Discord onboarding is realistic for a part-timer; Cohere Labs Catalyst grant terms; Marin's contributor experiment process (could our studies run under their preregistration model?); the "[Selecting LMs for Social Science: Start Small, Start Open, and Validate](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.10926)" paper (surfaced but unread — likely a bridge audience for this work); whether flips/KL metrics correlate with *human-perceived* quality changes (no study found).

## Fit notes

This space's core activity — building controlled behavioral comparisons and defensible divergence metrics for model variants — is evaluation methodology applied to open checkpoints, which matches a profile with a published LLM-as-a-judge/hallucination-detection study, strong data-pipeline skills, and classical-ML statistics. The compute envelope is real, not aspirational: the canonical tools (Pythia suites, QLoRA/bitsandbytes, GGUF quants, FSDP+QLoRA) were built specifically so this work runs on one or two consumer GPUs or short rented-GPU bursts, and the field contains verified solo and ≤4-author publications (TRM; Wang's position paper; MSR India's flips paper; localbench). Standing collaboration on-ramps that don't require employment exist at EleutherAI (Discord, flat volunteer structure), Cohere Labs Open Science Community (open application, grants), and Marin (GitHub-mediated experiments). The gap list skews toward measurement and harm-relevant behavior (multilingual degradation, safety under quantization, distillation loss reporting) — impact-first framings rather than product framings.
