# Problem 08 — Model Architecture Research (assessed for a non-architecture-specialist)

**One-line:** Frontier architecture design is lab-scale by construction, but three adjacent niches — small-scale architecture *science*, independent stress-testing of architecture *claims*, and architecture-*behavior* studies — have a real solo track record and live gaps.

Grounding doc (read first, verified against primary sources): [`ai-research-map/02-architectures-and-training/post-transformer-architectures.md`](../../ai-research-map/02-architectures-and-training/post-transformer-architectures.md)

---

## The problem, concretely

**What's at stake.** Softmax attention costs grow with the square of context length; the field is racing to replace or dilute it with sub-quadratic primitives (SSMs, gated linear attention, RNN revivals, test-time-training memory). The current production answer is *hybrids* — a few full-attention layers among many linear ones — shipping in Kimi Linear ([arXiv:2510.26692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26692)), MiniMax-M1 ([arXiv:2506.13585](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13585)), Qwen3-Next ([weights, no paper](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Instruct)), Hunyuan-TurboS ([arXiv:2505.15431](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15431)), Falcon-H1 ([arXiv:2507.22448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22448)).

**Why frontier design work is mostly closed to a solo researcher (facts, not vibes):**
- Decisive ablations are run at *matched trillion-token budgets*: Kimi Linear's headline claim ("first linear-attention hybrid to beat full attention") rests on matched **1.4T-token** training runs; Hunyuan-TurboS trained on **16T tokens**; MiniMax-M1's RL phase alone used **512 H800s for 3 weeks (~$0.53M)** (all from the papers above, via the map doc).
- The work is entangled with kernel engineering (Mamba-2's SSD block algorithm, [arXiv:2405.21060](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.21060)) and training-stability firefighting at scale (MiniMax-M1 and Hunyuan-TurboS both report needing FP32 for SSM/output states).
- This is a documented structural trend, not an impression: Besiroglu et al., ["The Compute Divide in Machine Learning"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02452), shows academic-only teams' representation shrinking precisely in compute-intensive topics like foundation models.

**The measurable sub-problems that remain open at small scale:**
1. **The recall/state-capacity wall** — pure sub-quadratic models fail exact recall as context grows (RWKV-7 passkey collapse past ~28K, [arXiv:2504.21463](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21463); Mamba-2/TTT/DeltaNet fail S-NIAH past ~8K, [arXiv:2501.00663](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00663)). *Where exactly* each new primitive hits the wall is measurable at <1B params.
2. **Claim verification** — headline claims (e.g. "outperforms full attention," "10× throughput") are almost all self-reported by the releasing lab; Qwen3-Next shipped with **no paper at all**. Independent behavioral evaluation is a distinct, tractable sub-problem.
3. **Trick transfer** — do architecture/training tricks discovered at one scale, dataset, and loss target survive changes to any of these? The one rigorous audit that exists (optimizers) found most claimed 2× speedups shrink to ≤1.4× under fair tuning ([arXiv:2509.02046](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02046)).
4. **Architecture → behavior mapping** — how a fixed-size recurrent state vs. a KV cache changes *behavior* (copying fidelity, in-context learning, state tracking, hallucination under long-context retrieval) is only partially charted, mostly for 2024-era Mamba, not the 2025–26 production primitives (KDA, Gated DeltaNet variants).

## The research field around it

Four sub-areas with different maturity:

- **Frontier primitive design + scaling (mature, lab-owned).** SSMs and duality theory (Mamba-2/SSD, [arXiv:2405.21060](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.21060)); production hybrids (Kimi Linear, MiniMax-M1, Qwen3-Next, Hunyuan-TurboS, Falcon-H1); test-time-training memory (Titans [arXiv:2501.00663](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00663), Nested Learning/"Hope" [arXiv:2512.24695](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24695)). Contested design choices — interleaved vs. parallel hybrid layout, fine-grained vs. scalar gating — are ablated in-house at scale.
- **Small-scale architecture science (established methodology, open to individuals).** Synthetic-task probing that predicts scale behavior: Zoology showed associative recall accounts for **>82% of the perplexity gap** between sub-quadratic models and attention, using a small-model playground ([Hazy Research blog](https://hazyresearch.stanford.edu/blog/2023-12-11-zoology1-analysis), [arXiv:2312.04927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04927)); MAD built synthetic "unit tests" that correlate with compute-optimal perplexity and used them to design better hybrids ([arXiv:2403.17844](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17844)); Physics of Language Models Part 4.1 uses fully synthetic pretraining to isolate architectural effects and proposes Canon layers ([SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5240330), [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/PhysicsLM4)). The **speedrun tradition** operationalizes this as leaderboards: [modded-nanogpt](https://github.com/KellerJordan/modded-nanogpt) (84 records, 45 min → **1.32 min** as of May 2026), [cifar10-airbench](https://github.com/KellerJordan/cifar10-airbench), and [Marin speedrun](http://marin.community/blog/2025/05/19/announcement/) (multi-budget tiers). The **BabyLM Challenge** (4th edition at EMNLP 2026, tracks at 10M/100M words + new multilingual track) explicitly lists "architecture innovation" in scope ([2026 CFP](https://www.alphaxiv.org/overview/2602.20092v2), [2025 proceedings](https://aclanthology.org/events/babylm-2025/)).
- **Reproduction / stress-testing of architecture claims (small but respected).** Theory + small experiments deflating claims: "The Illusion of State in State-Space Models" proved SSMs are stuck in TC⁰ like transformers, so their "state" advantage for state-tracking is illusory ([arXiv:2404.08819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08819)); "Repeat After Me" proved and measured a copying gap from fixed-size states ([arXiv:2402.01032](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01032)); NVIDIA's matched-data 8B Mamba-vs-Transformer study confirmed pure Mamba lags on MMLU/phonebook-recall while hybrids win ([arXiv:2406.07887](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07887)); "Fantastic Pretraining Optimizers" audited 10 optimizers under fair tuning and cut claimed speedups roughly in half ([arXiv:2509.02046](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02046)).
- **Architecture-behavior studies (young, fragmented).** ICL comparisons: Mamba matches transformers on many ICL tasks but SSMs fail retrieval/decision-tree tasks while transformers fail sparse parity, motivating the MambaFormer hybrid ([arXiv:2402.03170](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03170), [arXiv:2402.04248](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.04248)). Long-context recall curves per architecture exist in scattered form (RULER numbers in each hybrid's own paper). Behavior-level measures (faithfulness, hallucination under retrieval, instruction following) by architecture are largely absent — see gaps.

## Who does this work

- **Frontier hybrid design:** Moonshot, MiniMax, Alibaba Qwen, Tencent, TII (state-funded UAE institute — Falcon-H1), Google Research (Titans/Hope), NVIDIA (hybrid empirical study, [arXiv:2406.07887](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07887)). Company-funded; closed to outsiders except via open weights.
- **Academic groups doing small-scale architecture science:** Stanford Hazy Research (Chris Ré — Zoology/Based/MAD lineage, [blog](https://hazyresearch.stanford.edu/blog/2023-12-11-zoology0-intro)); Princeton/CMU (Tri Dao, Albert Gu); NYU + AI2 (Merrill/Petty/Sabharwal — expressivity stress tests); Harvard Kempner (Jelassi/Kakade — copying); Meta FAIR (Allen-Zhu's Physics of LM, [site](https://physics.allen-zhu.com/part-4-architecture-design/part-4-1)); University of Oslo LTG (BabyLM winners, "fully funded by the University of Oslo" per [arXiv:2410.24159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24159)). Grant/university funding.
- **Open community labs:** **Marin** (Stanford CRFM/HAI) runs model development fully in the open — anyone can propose, review, and run experiments via GitHub, and its speedrun "accepts submissions at multiple compute budgets" ([announcement](http://marin.community/blog/2025/05/19/announcement/), [repo issues](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/1981)). **RWKV** was initiated and led by an individual (Bo Peng / BlinkDL) and is now a Linux Foundation AI & Data incubation project with a Discord-organized contributor community ([LF page](https://lfaidata.foundation/projects/rwkv/), [repo](https://github.com/BlinkDL/RWKV-LM)). Nonprofit/foundation + volunteer funding models.
- **Individuals (the speedrun tradition):** Keller Jordan (modded-nanogpt + cifar10-airbench; the **Muon optimizer** debuted as speedrun record #3, Oct 2024, per the [repo](https://github.com/KellerJordan/modded-nanogpt)); tysam-code (hlb-CIFAR10, the prior CIFAR speed record per [arXiv:2404.00498](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00498)); Tyler Romero (solo worklog on 2×RTX 4090, 8.13h → 2.55h, [worklog](https://www.tylerromero.com/posts/nanogpt-speedrun-worklog/)); record contributors like @sisovicm (current 1.320-min record). Hobbyist/self-funded; **Prime Intellect** (a company) sponsors validation runs for record attempts and hosts a [leaderboard](https://app.primeintellect.ai/speedrun/nanogpt).
- **How outsiders contribute, concretely:** PRs to modded-nanogpt (rules: same data pipeline, p<0.01 significance on the loss target, faster on identical 8×H100 hardware — [repo](https://github.com/KellerJordan/modded-nanogpt)); GitHub experiment proposals to Marin at any compute budget; BabyLM shared-task submissions (10M/100M-word budgets are single-GPU-trainable; [2026 CFP](https://www.alphaxiv.org/overview/2602.20092v2)); RWKV community channels; workshop/arXiv reproduction papers.

## Published exemplars (small-team/solo favored)

1. **modded-nanogpt speedrun leaderboard** — Keller Jordan + community, 2024–26 ([repo](https://github.com/KellerJordan/modded-nanogpt)). 84 records took GPT-2(124M)-to-3.28-loss from 45 min to 1.32 min; individual PRs contributed rotary/QK-norm, FP8, attention-window scheduling, multi-token prediction, bigram hash embeddings. Mattered because it produced the Muon optimizer and became the field's de-facto shared testbed for small-scale training/architecture tricks.
2. **"94% on CIFAR-10 in 3.29 Seconds on a Single GPU"** — Keller Jordan, solo author ([arXiv:2404.00498](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00498); now 2.59s with Muon per [repo](https://github.com/KellerJordan/cifar10-airbench)). Mattered as proof that one person on one GPU can do publishable, rigorous training-science, and as explicit infrastructure for cheap experiments ("airbench").
3. **RWKV** — initiated and led by one independent researcher, Bo Peng, grown into an LF AI & Data project ([arXiv:2305.13048](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13048); RWKV-7 [arXiv:2503.14456](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14456); [LF page](https://lfaidata.foundation/projects/rwkv/)). Mattered as the existence proof that an individual-led architecture line can stay competitive for years.
4. **GPT-BERT ("GPT or BERT: why not both?")** — 2 authors, University of Oslo ([arXiv:2410.24159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24159), [code](https://github.com/ltgoslo/gpt-bert)). Won *both* BabyLM 2024 tracks by fusing masked and causal objectives in one stack, trained on ≤100M words. Mattered as the canonical "small budget, real architecture contribution" result.
5. **"The Illusion of State in State-Space Models"** — 3 authors, NYU/AI2, ICML 2024 ([arXiv:2404.08819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08819)). Theory + small experiments showing SSMs cannot express permutation composition (TC⁰-bounded), deflating the "recurrence gives real state" narrative with near-zero compute. Mattered as a template for compute-free claim-stress-testing.
6. **"Repeat After Me"** — 4 authors, Harvard, ICML 2024 ([arXiv:2402.01032](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01032), [code](https://github.com/sjelassi/transformers_ssm_copy)). Proved 2-layer transformers copy exponential-length strings while fixed-state models can't; verified on pretrained models. Mattered because it turned an architecture property into a cheap behavioral probe anyone can run.
7. **Zoology / Based** — Hazy Research, small academic team ([arXiv:2312.04927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04927), [blog series](https://hazyresearch.stanford.edu/blog/2023-12-11-zoology1-analysis), [Based](https://hazyresearch.stanford.edu/blog/2024-03-03-based)). Quantified that associative recall drives >82% of the sub-quadratic quality gap, then *designed a new architecture from the diagnosis*. Mattered as the model workflow: synthetic diagnosis → architecture fix.
8. **"Fantastic Pretraining Optimizers and Where to Find Them"** — 4 authors, Stanford ([arXiv:2509.02046](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02046); follow-up [arXiv:2606.16899](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.16899)). Fair-tuning audit found claimed 2× optimizer speedups are really ≤1.4× and shrink with scale (Muon ~1.1× at 1.2B). Mattered as the exemplar of *stress-testing training claims* — the same audit style has not been applied to most architecture claims.

## Where the gaps are

- **No independent verification of the 2025–26 hybrid headline claims.** Kimi Linear's "first linear architecture to outperform full attention" ([arXiv:2510.26692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26692)) is self-reported; Qwen3-Next shipped weights with **no paper** ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Instruct)). The independent-eval literature (Illusion of State, Repeat After Me, NVIDIA study) targeted 2024-era Mamba/S4 — I found no third-party behavioral stress-test of KDA or the Gated-DeltaNet-family primitives in this research pass. ⚠️ absence claim: "not found," not "doesn't exist."
- **Trick-transfer is unaudited beyond optimizers.** The Stanford optimizer audit ([arXiv:2509.02046](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02046)) showed baseline undertuning inflates claims; the 84 modded-nanogpt records optimize *one* loss target on *one* dataset at *one* scale ([repo](https://github.com/KellerJordan/modded-nanogpt)) — whether records' architectural components (bigram hash embeddings, paired-head attention, MUDD skips) help at other scales/targets is untested publicly. MAD's synthetics-predict-scaling claim ([arXiv:2403.17844](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17844)) was validated on 2024 primitives, not the current gated-delta family.
- **Recall-wall cartography is stale.** The passkey/S-NIAH break points are documented for RWKV-7 (~28K) and Mamba-2/TTT/DeltaNet (~8K) ([arXiv:2504.21463](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21463), [arXiv:2501.00663](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00663)) but not, per this pass, for channel-wise-gated KDA vs. scalar-gated GDN at matched small scale — exactly the contested design axis the map doc identifies. ⚠️ absence claim.
- **Behavior-level consequences are uncharted.** ICL-task differences are known ([arXiv:2402.03170](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03170), [arXiv:2402.04248](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.04248)), but no work found connects architecture class to hallucination rate / faithfulness under long-context RAG-style use — despite lossy fixed-size states being a mechanistically plausible cause. ⚠️ inferred gap (mechanism read from papers; the missing study is my inference from not finding one).
- **The speedrun niche is being automated.** Prime Intellect is running autonomous-agent speedrun research ([auto-nanogpt](https://www.primeintellect.ai/auto-nanogpt)) and frontier coding agents have been benchmarked against the human record with 512 H100-hour budgets ([NanoGPT-Bench](https://github.com/IntologyAI/NanoGPT-Bench)) — human comparative advantage may shift from trick-finding to claim-auditing and behavior science. ⚠️ partly inferred from search-result summaries; repos opened only via search.

## What a solo researcher could do here

(Study shapes with cost/method facts; no ranking.)

1. **Speedrun-record component audit.** Take the last N modded-nanogpt records, re-ablate each component at a *different* loss target/dataset/scale, and report which gains survive — the optimizer-audit methodology ([arXiv:2509.02046](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02046)) applied to architecture tricks. The current record run is ~1.3 min on 8×H100; hundreds of full runs fit in a $500 rental budget, and the repo's own p<0.01 significance rule ([repo](https://github.com/KellerJordan/modded-nanogpt)) supplies the statistical framing. Tyler Romero's 2×4090 worklog shows the consumer-hardware variant ([worklog](https://www.tylerromero.com/posts/nanogpt-speedrun-worklog/)).
2. **Recall-wall map for the 2025–26 primitives.** Zoology/MAD-style synthetic suite (associative recall, copying, permutation composition per [arXiv:2402.01032](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01032)/[arXiv:2404.08819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08819)) run at matched <400M scale across KDA-style channel-wise gating vs. GDN scalar gating vs. Mamba-2 vs. RWKV-7 — directly testing the contested gating axis at costs the Zoology team already demonstrated are small-lab-scale ([arXiv:2312.04927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04927)).
3. **Independent behavioral stress-test of a released hybrid.** Open weights exist across sizes (Falcon-H1 0.5B–34B [arXiv:2507.22448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22448); RWKV-7; Qwen3-Next). Run the published probe batteries (copying, phonebook/NIAH per [arXiv:2406.07887](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07887), state-tracking) as *inference-only* evaluation — no training compute — and compare against each model's own claims. This is the eval-methodology skill set applied to architecture.
4. **Architecture × hallucination study.** Matched-scale models of different architecture classes evaluated on RAG-style long-context tasks with an LLM-as-judge faithfulness pipeline — a direct extension of the researcher's published judge work to the open architecture-behavior gap above. Frontier-API judge + local small models keeps it in budget.
5. **BabyLM 2026 architecture entry.** The 10M/100M-word tracks are explicitly architecture-innovation-friendly and single-GPU trainable; the 2024 dual-track winner was a 2-author university project ([arXiv:2410.24159](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24159); [2026 CFP](https://www.alphaxiv.org/overview/2602.20092v2)). A hybrid-primitive entry (e.g., linear-attention layer mixes at BabyLM scale) would be novel to the venue. ⚠️ "novel to the venue" unverified — 2026 submissions not yet public.
6. **Marin open-lab experiment.** Propose and run an architecture-comparison experiment through Marin's GitHub process, which accepts speedrun submissions "at multiple compute budgets" ([announcement](http://marin.community/blog/2025/05/19/announcement/)) — a path to co-authorship-style credit inside an academic open lab without employment.

## What we still don't know

- Whether Kimi Linear's matched-scale "beats full attention" result replicates under independent training or even independent *evaluation* (no third-party check found).
- Whether MAD-style synthetic unit tests remain predictive for the gated-delta primitive family (validated only on 2024 primitives).
- Whether nanoGPT-speedrun components transfer across scale/dataset/loss-target (no published audit found; optimizer analogue suggests substantial shrinkage).
- Where KDA/GDN variants hit the recall wall relative to Mamba-2/RWKV-7 at matched scale.
- Whether architecture class measurably shifts hallucination/faithfulness behavior at matched capability (no study found; mechanism plausible).
- How fast agent-driven speedrunning ([auto-nanogpt](https://www.primeintellect.ai/auto-nanogpt), [NanoGPT-Bench](https://github.com/IntologyAI/NanoGPT-Bench)) erodes the human trick-finding niche, and whether claim-auditing resists automation similarly. ⚠️ speculative framing.
- Whether an 80B-A3B-class hybrid (Qwen3-Next) can be *meaningfully* stress-tested inference-only within a $500 budget (quantized local/rented inference feasibility unverified).
- BabyLM 2026 deadlines and evaluation pipeline details (CFP fetched did not include dates).

## Fit notes

The profile's strongest verified asset — published LLM-as-a-judge evaluation methodology — maps onto the two niches with the clearest documented gaps: independent stress-testing of self-reported architecture claims and architecture-behavior measurement, both of which are inference-heavy rather than training-heavy. The $500/study budget covers speedrun-scale training (current record ~1.3 min on 8×H100) and <400M-param synthetic-task ablations, the scales at which Zoology, MAD, and the BabyLM winners operated. Data-pipeline skills apply directly to building synthetic-task harnesses and eval batteries; no CUDA-kernel expertise is required for any of the six study shapes above, though it is required for frontier primitive design. Precedent exists for every participation channel named (PR-based leaderboards, shared tasks, open-lab GitHub experiments, solo arXiv papers).
