# AI Evaluation & Benchmarking: The Institutional Landscape (mid-2026)

Brief 01 in the evaluation-field series. All claims below come from pages opened during research (early July 2026) unless marked **⚠️ unverified**. "READ" = seen on a source page; "inferred" flagged where applicable.

---

## The shape of this space — what the work actually consists of

The field has stratified into distinguishable sub-areas, each with different institutions attached:

1. **Dangerous-capability / safety evaluations** — pre-deployment testing of frontier models for autonomy, cyber-offense, bio/chem uplift, scheming. Players: [METR](https://metr.org/about), [Apollo Research](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/blog/apollo-update-may-2026/), [Palisade](https://palisaderesearch.org/about), [UK AISI](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/evals-bounty), [US CAISI/NIST](https://www.nist.gov/caisi).
2. **Capability benchmarks & leaderboards** — static and live rankings of general capability. Players: [Epoch AI (FrontierMath)](https://epoch.ai/frontiermath), [LMArena/Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMArena), [Scale Labs/SEAL (HLE, SWE-Bench Pro)](https://scale.com/blog/scales-next-era-building-for-2026), [Berkeley BFCL](https://sky.cs.berkeley.edu/project/berkeley-function-calling-leaderboard/), [CAIS (HLE)](https://agi.safe.ai/).
3. **Evaluation infrastructure / harnesses** — the software everyone runs evals on: [EleutherAI lm-eval-harness](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness), [UK AISI Inspect](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/) (200+ pre-built evals), [HF lighteval](https://github.com/huggingface/lighteval), [Stanford HELM](https://github.com/stanford-crfm/helm) (now maintenance-mode), [Princeton HAL harness](https://github.com/princeton-pli/hal-harness).
4. **Evaluation science / meta-evaluation** — treating evals themselves as the object of study: validity, statistics, cost, reproducibility. Players: [EvalEval Coalition](https://evalevalai.com/), Princeton ([HAL paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11977)), the [NeurIPS Evaluations & Datasets track](https://blog.neurips.cc/2026/03/23/introducing-the-evaluations-datasets-track-at-neurips-2026/) (renamed from Datasets & Benchmarks for 2026 explicitly because "evaluation itself becomes an object of scientific study"), position papers like [Toward an Evaluation Science for Generative AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05336).
5. **Standards & assurance** — consortium/government-grade certification: [MLCommons AILuminate + Global Assurance Program](https://mlcommons.org/2026/02/ailuminate-global-assurance/), [NIST's International Network consensus on automated-evaluation practices (Feb 2026)](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/02/international-network-advanced-ai-measurement-evaluation-and-science), [EU AI Office external-evaluator qualification work](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/events/call-participants-workshop-qualification-requirements-external-evaluators-gpai-models-systemic-risk).
6. **Demonstrations-as-evidence** — empirical demos of concerning behavior aimed at policymakers/public rather than leaderboards (Palisade's shutdown-resistance and CTF work).

A structural shift READ across multiple sources: evaluation is professionalizing and bifurcating — VC-scale money at the leaderboard end (LMArena at $1.7B), government institutionalization in the middle (CAISI, AISI, EU AI Office), and an under-resourced science/commons layer (HELM in maintenance mode, EvalEval arguing [eval costs are the new bottleneck](https://huggingface.co/blog/evaleval/eval-costs-bottleneck)).

## Who does this work

### Safety-eval nonprofits

**[METR](https://metr.org/about)** — nonprofit; "develop scientific methods to assess catastrophic risks stemming from AI systems' autonomous capabilities." Funding (READ on about page): philanthropic grants from The Audacious Project (~$17M of a [$38M "Canary" project with RAND](https://metr.org/blog/2024-10-09-new-support-through-the-audacious-project/)), Sijbrandij Foundation, Pew Charitable Trusts, Schmidt Sciences, Packard Foundation, individual donors (Dylan Field, Geoff Ralston, Steve Newman), pooled funds via Longview Philanthropy; plus a **technical assistance contract with the European AI Office**. Explicitly takes **no money from AI companies** (uses free compute credits only). Size: ~40+ staff on the about page. Getting in: [careers page](https://metr.org/careers); the famous **task bounty ($300/professional-hour, >$100k paid out) is paused indefinitely** ([task dev guide](https://taskdev.metr.org/bounty/)), but METR states bounties were a hiring pipeline and good submissions could fast-track applications.

**[Apollo Research](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/)** — began fiscally sponsored by Rethink Priorities (501c3), **converted to a Public Benefit Corporation in Jan 2026** ([announcement](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/blog/apollo-research-is-becoming-a-pbc/)) explicitly to grow faster and diversify funding. Focus shifted from scheming *evals* to "**Science of Scheming**," plus agent monitoring and governance ([May 2026 update](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/blog/apollo-update-may-2026/)). Offices: London + SF (SF growing 3→10+ in 2026), DC office opened June 2026. Hiring rounds expected Q3–Q4 2026 across research, policy, national-security roles ([careers](https://www.apolloresearch.ai/careers/)). Earlier grant funding visible on [Manifund](https://manifund.org/projects/apollo-research-scale-up-interpretability--behavioral-model-evals-research). Total headcount: **⚠️ unverified**.

**[Palisade Research](https://palisaderesearch.org/about)** — small nonprofit (9 staff listed on about page), founded 2023 by Jeffrey Ladish (ex-Anthropic infosec). Mission: help institutions avoid "permanent disempowerment by strategic AI agents"; known for shutdown-resistance, chess-hacking, and GPT-5-in-CTF demos covered by Time/WSJ/BBC. Funding: donations; [SFF matches donations 1:1 up to $1.1M](https://palisaderesearch.org/blog/ai-control-palisade-2026); had **~7 months runway as of Dec 2025** — a live example of how thin nonprofit eval funding is. Hires via a public Gusto job board; takes donations via Open Collective/every.org.

**[EleutherAI](https://www.eleuther.ai/)** — 501c3 research institute (since early 2023) that grew out of a Discord collective; maintains **[lm-evaluation-harness](https://github.com/EleutherAI/lm-evaluation-harness)**, the de-facto standard open eval framework (used by HF's Open LLM Leaderboard). ### Government

**[UK AI Security Institute (AISI)](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/)** — UK government (DSIT). Built **[Inspect](https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/)**, the open-source eval framework (with Meridian Labs; 200+ pre-built evals), and **[Inspect Evals](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/inspect-evals)**, a community repo maintained with **[Arcadia Impact](https://www.arcadiaimpact.org/about)** (via its AI Safety Engineering Taskforce) and the Vector Institute — from May 2026 community submissions go through a [register process](https://github.com/UKGovernmentBEIS/inspect_evals) (GitHub issue + arXiv link + code). Ran an **[evals bounty](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/evals-bounty)**: up to £200k, £2k compute at stage 2, £100–200/development-hour (≈£3k–15k per accepted task) — **⚠️ unverified whether a 2026 round is currently open** (the announced deadline seen was Nov 30 of the launch year). Also publishes an [Autonomous Systems Evaluation Standard](https://ukgovernmentbeis.github.io/as-evaluation-standard/). Headcount: **⚠️ unverified**.

**[US CAISI (NIST)](https://www.nist.gov/caisi)** — Center for AI Standards and Innovation (Commerce/NIST; successor to US AISI). 40+ completed model assessments incl. unreleased models; classified evals via interagency TRAINS Taskforce; **pre-deployment testing agreements with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Microsoft, xAI** ([May 2026](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2026/05/05/advancing-ai-evaluation-with-the-center-for-ai-standards-us-and-innovation-and-the-ai-security-institute-uk/)); published [DeepSeek V4 Pro evaluation](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/05/caisi-evaluation-deepseek-v4-pro); launched the [AI Agent Standards Initiative (Feb 2026)](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-nist-ai-agent-standards-federal-framework/); [MOU with GSA for federal-procurement eval science](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/03/caisi-signs-mou-gsa-boost-ai-evaluation-science-federal-procurement-through). Convenes the [International Network for Advanced AI Measurement, Evaluation, and Science](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/02/international-network-advanced-ai-measurement-evaluation-and-science) (consensus practices for automated evaluations, Feb 2026). Involvement: federal hiring; standards working groups.

**[EU AI Office](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai)** — regulator. The [GPAI Code of Practice](https://code-of-practice.ai/) obliges signatories to give "sufficient numbers of independent external evaluators" complimentary access to their most advanced models. Currently defining **qualification and independence requirements for external evaluators** ([workshop ~15 July 2026](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/events/call-participants-workshop-qualification-requirements-external-evaluators-gpai-models-systemic-risk)) and will launch a **call to expand EU evaluation capacity, operational by 2027**. Contracts out technical work (METR holds a technical-assistance contract). This is creating a *regulated market* for third-party evaluators — a structural change worth tracking.

## Published exemplars (small-team-weighted)

1. **[Adding Error Bars to Evals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00640)** — Evan Miller, **solo author** (Anthropic), Nov 2024. Treats evals as experiments drawn from a super-population; gives formulas for CIs, paired model comparisons, power analysis. Landed because the entire field was reporting point scores with no statistics; now routinely cited in eval methodology work. Proof that a single methodologically-sharp person can move field norms.
2. **[Holistic Agent Leaderboard](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11977)** — Stroebl, Kapoor, Narayanan (3-person core, Princeton), ICLR 2026. Cost-controlled, reproducible agent evals; 21,730 rollouts; [live leaderboard + reliability dashboard](https://hal.cs.princeton.edu/). Landed because agent leaderboards ignored cost and variance; HAL made both non-optional.
3. **[Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Tasks](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14499v1)** — METR, Mar 2025 (updated [Time Horizon 1.1, Jan 2026](https://metr.org/blog/2026-1-29-time-horizon-1-1/)). The 50%-task-completion time horizon, doubling ~every 7 months. Arguably the single most-cited eval result for AGI timelines; even has [its own Epoch tracking page](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/metr-time-horizons). Shows a *metric design* contribution can outweigh any individual benchmark.
4. **[The Leaderboard Illusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20879)** — Cohere Labs + AI2/Princeton/Stanford/Waterloo/UW, Apr 2025. 68-page audit of Chatbot Arena: undisclosed private variant testing (27 Meta variants pre-Llama-4), data-access asymmetry (~20% of battle data each to OpenAI/Google), overfitting effects. Forced [a public LMArena response](https://arena.ai/blog/our-response/) and policy changes. Exemplar of *auditing the evaluators* — no model training required.
5. **[FrontierMath](https://epoch.ai/frontiermath)** — Epoch AI (small core team + contracted mathematicians). Expert-written unpublished math problems; became the reference hard-math benchmark. Also a cautionary tale twice over: the [OpenAI funding-disclosure controversy](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/19/ai-benchmarking-organization-criticized-for-waiting-to-disclose-funding-from-openai/) and a [June 2026 update fixing errors in 42% of problems](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/frontiermath-tier-4-v2).
6. **[Berkeley Function-Calling Leaderboard](https://gorilla.cs.berkeley.edu/leaderboard.html)** — grad-student-led (Shishir Patil et al., Sky Lab). From a course-project-scale effort (Feb 2024) to the industry-standard tool-use benchmark, now [a journal-track paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=2GmDdhBdDk).
7. **[Every Eval Ever](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.14516)** — EvalEval Coalition, Feb 2026. Unifying schema + community repository making eval results traceable/reproducible; results surfaced [directly on HF model pages](https://huggingface.co/blog/eee-community-evals). Exemplar of coalition-style infrastructure work with open co-authorship.
8. **[Toward an Evaluation Science for Generative AI Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05336)** — Weidinger, Raji, Wallach, Mitchell, et al. (10 authors across DeepMind/Berkeley/MSR/HF/Stanford), 2025. The agenda-setting position paper: static benchmarks have validity problems, audits don't scale, borrow from aerospace/pharma safety engineering. The intellectual frame most 2026 eval-science work cites.

## Where the gaps are (named, sourced)

- **Cost is now the binding constraint, and it concentrates power.** The [EvalEval cost analysis (Apr 2026)](https://huggingface.co/blog/evaleval/eval-costs-bottleneck) documents $40k for one HAL sweep, ~$9.5k per PaperBench run, 33× cost spreads on identical tasks, and argues "whoever can pay for the evaluation gets to write the leaderboard." Named calls-to-action: standardized artifact sharing, single-vs-repeated-run reliability measurement, cost-aware Pareto leaderboards, and **benchmark compression for agent evals** (static benchmarks compress 100–200×; agent benchmarks only 2–3.5×) — each is a tractable research problem.
- **Statistical malpractice is still the norm.** [Miller 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00640) shows the basics were missing; follow-ons like [How to Correctly Report LLM-as-a-Judge Evaluations](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.21140) (Nov 2025) show judge-based evals in particular lack calibrated error reporting. Directly adjacent to the researcher's published LLM-as-judge work.
- **Validity/benchmark quality control.** FrontierMath's [42%-of-problems error correction (June 2026)](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/frontiermath-tier-4-v2) happened *after* the benchmark had been shaping frontier-lab narratives — the field has no routine independent error-auditing of benchmarks. The [Inspect Evals maintenance paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06893) documents the same problem from the repo-maintainer side (quality assurance of community evals is hard, undervalued labor).
- **Leaderboard governance.** [The Leaderboard Illusion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20879) named specific mechanisms (private variant testing, sampling asymmetry, silent retraction). LMArena [disputed parts](https://arena.ai/blog/our-response/), but its commercialization ($1.7B valuation) keeps the referee-with-revenue conflict live. Independent audit of eval platforms remains nearly unoccupied.
- **Funder independence and disclosure norms.** Epoch/OpenAI (FrontierMath), Scale/Meta (49% stake), and Apollo's nonprofit→PBC conversion all show the independence question is unsettled; METR's "no AI-company money" stance is the exception, and even METR notes independent funding was "crucial" ([about page](https://metr.org/about)). No standard disclosure regime exists yet — Evaluation Cards ([EvalEval](https://huggingface.co/blog/evaleval/evaluation-cards-launch)) is an early attempt.
- **Sustainability of the academic commons.** [HELM entered maintenance mode June 1, 2026](https://github.com/stanford-crfm/helm); HF's Open LLM Leaderboard is archived; METR's task bounty is paused. The infrastructure layer everyone depends on is chronically under-maintained (READ across those three pages; the synthesis is inferred).
- **A regulated third-party-evaluator market is forming with no agreed qualifications.** The EU AI Office is [actively drafting evaluator independence/qualification requirements (July 2026 workshop)](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/events/call-participants-workshop-qualification-requirements-external-evaluators-gpai-models-systemic-risk) and plans an EU eval-capacity call operational by 2027; NIST's International Network published only initial [consensus areas for automated evaluations (Feb 2026)](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/02/international-network-advanced-ai-measurement-evaluation-and-science). Who counts as a qualified evaluator is an open institutional question.
- **GCR-relevant benchmark demand outstrips supply.** Open Phil's (now Coefficient Giving) [capability-evals RFP](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/request-for-proposals-improving-capability-evaluations/) named three funding gaps — more demanding GCR-relevant benchmarks, better evaluation science, third-party model access/infrastructure — before closing to applications (Apr 2025; proposals redirected to the [AI Governance general RFP](https://www.openphilanthropy.org/request-for-proposals-ai-governance/)).
