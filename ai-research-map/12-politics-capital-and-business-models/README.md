# 12 · Politics, Capital & Business Models

AI research doesn't happen in a vacuum — it's shaped by who funds it, what business models
attract that funding, and the geopolitics of compute and regulation. This branch maps the
*money and power* dimension.

## The shape of this area

Three dynamics dominate mid-2026:

1. **Capital is betting on a "research era."** Record sums flow to labs with little or no
   public output (SSI ~$32B valuation, Thinking Machines, Recursive Superintelligence
   $4.65B) on the thesis that the *next* breakthrough is algorithmic, not just bigger
   datacenters.
2. **Business models are fragmenting.** Beyond the frontier-API model, distinct fundable
   models emerged: open-weight, inference/infrastructure, agents-as-product, AI-for-science
   "labs," world models, and interpretability-as-a-service.
3. **Compute and power became geopolitics.** Export controls, sovereign-AI programs, the
   HBM-memory chokepoint, and a hyperscaler nuclear-energy buildout now gate who can do
   frontier research at all.

> **📦 Why this belongs in a *research* map** — funding shapes which research directions get
> explored. The shift to reasoning, the open-weight wave, the world-models boom, and the
> efficiency focus of compute-constrained labs are all partly *downstream of money and
> policy*.
