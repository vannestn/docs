## The shape of this area

Robotics **converged on dual-system VLA architectures** (a slow VLM/reasoning module + a
fast diffusion/flow action module) across DeepMind, NVIDIA, Figure, and Physical
Intelligence, with 2025–26's theme being *open-world generalization* and *RL-based self-
improvement from experience*. **World models split into two camps**: generative/interactive
(Genie 3, Marble, Cosmos, Sora/Veo) vs. predictive/representation-space for planning
(V-JEPA 2, LeCun's new AMI Labs). Spatial intelligence / world models also became a
distinct, heavily-funded startup category — see
[10 · Emerging labs](../10-startups-and-nascent-fields/emerging-labs.md) and
[12 · Business models](../12-politics-capital-and-business-models/business-models.md).
