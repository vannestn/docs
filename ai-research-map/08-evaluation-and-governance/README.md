## The shape of this area

The defining tension: **benchmarks saturate within ~1 year of release** (ARC-AGI, SWE-bench,
FrontierMath, Humanity's Last Exam all followed the same arc), forcing a move to *dynamic,
contamination-resistant* evals and aggregate indices (Epoch's ECI). Meanwhile **sandbagging
and evaluation-awareness** threaten the validity of the whole eval-based governance regime
(if models underperform when tested, evals *underestimate* risk). Governments built real
evaluation capacity (UK AISI, US CAISI) and the EU AI Act's GPAI obligations entered force,
but independent scorecards find voluntary safety frameworks capture only a fraction of
recommended practice. The political/capital backdrop is in
[12 · Politics, Capital & Business Models](../12-politics-capital-and-business-models/).
