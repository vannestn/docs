# 11 · Emerging Application Subfields

Application areas that matured into *research fields of their own* — with dedicated
benchmarks, methods, and communities.

## The shape of this area

These started as "applications" and became "fields" once they developed their own *open
problems*. AI-for-software-engineering is the most mature (autonomous agents, dedicated
benchmarks, "code world models"). Memory and multi-agent coordination are earlier but
fast-moving. Several nascent interpretability ideas (persona vectors, introspection) sit
here because they emerged from studying deployed model *behavior*.
