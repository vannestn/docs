## The shape of this area

RL-with-verifiable-rewards (RLVR) is the proven workhorse behind reasoning models
(connect to [01 · Reasoning](../01-foundation-models-and-capabilities/reasoning-and-test-time-compute.md)),
and the open empirical question — *does RL create new capability or just amplify the base
model?* — is one of the field's most consequential live debates. At the frontier of
ambition sits **open-endedness**: self-improving agents (Darwin Gödel Machine),
algorithm-discovering systems (AlphaEvolve), and AI scientists — now attracting serious
capital (Recursive Superintelligence's $650M round). This is also where some of the
sharpest **safety** concerns about recursive self-improvement live.
