# Emerging Labs

Newer/smaller labs and what each is actually researching. (Funding details and the business-
model lens are in [12 · Business models](../12-politics-capital-and-business-models/business-models.md).)

## "Post-scaling research era" labs
- **Safe Superintelligence (SSI)** — Ilya Sutskever; ~$6B raised at ~$32B valuation, **zero
  papers/products**, betting that *algorithmic* breakthroughs (not scale) drive the next leap.
- **Thinking Machines Lab** — Mira Murati; first work on "interaction models" for real-time
  human-AI collaboration. [coverage](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/05/13/mira-muratis-thinking-machines-lab-introduces-interaction-models-a-native-multimodal-architecture-for-real-time-human-ai-collaboration/)
- **Reflection AI** — aiming to be a Western open-weight frontier lab (an "American DeepSeek").
- **AMI Labs** — Yann LeCun's new lab pursuing **JEPA / world models** (predict-in-
  representation-space, not generate pixels). See [06 · World models](../06-robotics-and-world-models/world-models-and-video.md).

## Alternative architectures
- **Inception Labs** — **Mercury**, the first commercial diffusion LLM (~10× faster decoding).
  [arXiv:2506.17298](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17298)
- **Liquid AI** — **LFM2**, hybrid models for *on-device* agents. [arXiv:2511.23404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23404)
- **Cartesia** — **Sonic** state-space-model audio (the Mamba founders); topped the speech
  arena. [Cartesia](https://www.cartesia.ai/launch/)

## World models / spatial intelligence
- **World Labs** (Fei-Fei Li) — **Marble** + **RTFM** persistent 3D worlds; raised $1B.
  [RTFM](https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/rtfm)
- **Decart** — **Oasis** real-time interactive world models. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/10/decarts-new-world-model-can-simulate-hours-of-photorealistic-driving-with-some-caveats/)

## Robotics
- **Physical Intelligence** — open-sourced **π0**; flow-matching robot policies. [pi](https://www.pi.website/blog/pi0)
- **Skild AI** — "omni-bodied" robot foundation model (~$1.4B raised).

## Autonomous science
- **Periodic Labs** — autonomous AI + robotic labs for materials/physics (~$300M seed).
- **Lila Sciences** — "AI Science Factories" (George Church; $350M Series A).
- **Sakana AI** — the **AI Scientist** and self-improvement (Darwin Gödel Machine, RSI Lab).

## Interpretability
- **Goodfire** — **Ember**, a hosted interpretability API (sparse autoencoders as a service);
  Anthropic's first outside investment. [Goodfire](https://www.goodfire.ai/blog/announcing-goodfire-ember)

## Generative media
- **Black Forest Labs** (image), **Runway / Luma / Pika** (video, pivoting toward world
  models), **Suno / Udio** (music, amid major-label licensing battles).

## Self-improvement / open-endedness
- **Recursive Superintelligence** — Rocktäschel/Clune/Tian; $650M at $4.65B for recursive
  self-improvement. See [04 · Open-endedness](../04-reinforcement-learning-and-open-endedness/open-endedness-and-self-improvement.md).

## State of research

**Best-performing now:** The *focused* labs with shipping products — Inception (diffusion
LMs), Cartesia (audio), World Labs (3D worlds), Physical Intelligence (robots), Goodfire
(interp) — have concrete, evaluable results.

**Promising but unproven:** The *stealth mega-funded* labs (SSI, Thinking Machines, Reflection,
AMI) are pure bets on talent and a research thesis, with little public output to evaluate.

**Open problems & weaknesses:** Valuations vastly exceed demonstrated results for several
labs — the "research era" thesis is unproven. Focused labs face the question of whether a
single capability is a defensible business. Many compete directly with frontier labs that
have far more compute.
