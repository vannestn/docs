## The shape of this area

A striking 2025–26 pattern: enormous funding for labs that have published little or nothing
yet, on the thesis that **algorithmic breakthroughs (not just scale) will drive the next
leap** — Ilya Sutskever's framing of a return to a "research era." Alongside them, a dense
ecosystem of *focused* labs: alternative architectures (diffusion LMs, state-space audio),
world models / spatial intelligence, interpretability-as-a-service, autonomous science labs,
and self-improving systems. The money/business-model angle is detailed in
[12 · Politics, Capital & Business Models](../12-politics-capital-and-business-models/).
