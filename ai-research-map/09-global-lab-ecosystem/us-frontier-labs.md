# US Frontier Labs

The best-capitalized labs setting the capability frontier. Their work appears
throughout this map; this page is a consolidated view of *who emphasizes what*.

## The players

- **OpenAI** — reasoning models (o-series), the GPT-5 unified router system, computer-use
  agents (Operator), Sora 2 video. Research emphasis: scaling reasoning + agents + product.
- **Anthropic** — Claude (coding/agents/computer use leadership), and the field's deepest
  **safety research** output: mechanistic interpretability (attribution graphs), model
  organisms of misalignment, control evals, Constitutional Classifiers. See
  [03 · Alignment](../03-alignment-interpretability-safety/).
- **Google DeepMind** — the broadest portfolio: Gemini (natively multimodal, Deep Think),
  AI-for-science (AlphaProof, AlphaFold/Isomorphic lineage, GenCast/WeatherNext), world
  models (Genie 3), robotics (Gemini Robotics), AlphaEvolve. The "research university" of
  frontier labs.
- **Meta (FAIR + Superintelligence Labs)** — open-weight Llama lineage, Byte Latent
  Transformer, Code World Model, V-JEPA world models. Note: Yann LeCun departed to found
  **AMI Labs** (JEPA/energy-based) — see [10 · Emerging labs](../10-startups-and-nascent-fields/emerging-labs.md).
- **xAI** — Grok (very long context, end-to-end tool-use RL), aggressive compute scaling.

## State of research

**Best-performing now:** These labs hold the capability frontier on reasoning, agents,
multimodal, and (DeepMind) AI-for-science. The proven SOTA on most hard benchmarks comes
from here.

**Promising but unproven:** Each is betting on a different "next thing" — OpenAI/Anthropic on
agents+reasoning, DeepMind on science+world models, Meta on open-weight + world models +
JEPA (now spun out).

**Open problems & weaknesses:** Closed models limit external scrutiny, and capability is
outpacing the safety commitments these same labs publish (see
[08 · Governance](../08-evaluation-and-governance/safety-institutes-and-governance.md)).
Pressure to ship strains the research/safety mission — a recurring theme in
[12 · Business models](../12-politics-capital-and-business-models/business-models.md).
