## The shape of this area

Two structural shifts define 2026: (1) **open-weight models reached the frontier in
biology** (Boltz-2, ESM C, Evo 2, STATE rival proprietary AF3-class systems), while the
absolute frontier (Isomorphic's drug-design engine) stays closed; (2) **autonomous "AI
science factory" labs** emerged as a business model — Periodic Labs, Lila Sciences, Sakana's
AI Scientist — pairing AI hypothesis generation with robotic experimentation. The headline
event: DeepMind's AlphaProof line resolving genuinely *open* Erdős/OEIS conjectures, moving
math AI past competition benchmarks into original research.
