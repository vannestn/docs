# Drug Discovery & the Clinical Pipeline

Where AI-designed molecules meet the hard reality of human clinical trials — the real test of
generative biology.

## Key directions & work

- **First positive Phase 2a for a fully generative-AI-discovered drug *and* AI-discovered
  target** — Insilico Medicine's **rentosertib** (a TNIK inhibitor) for idiopathic pulmonary
  fibrosis (scarring of the lungs) showed a real lung-function signal: the strongest single
  clinical milestone to date.
  [Nature Medicine 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03743-2)
- **Isomorphic Labs** (DeepMind spinout, AlphaFold-3-based) — 17 programs across oncology/
  immunology/cardiovascular, but had **dosed no human patient** as of early 2026; first-trial
  timing slipped from end-2025 to end-2026. [coverage](https://www.clinicaltrialsarena.com/news/isomorphic-labs-prepares-trials-ai-designed-drugs/)
- **The AI-originated pipeline grew to 170+ programs** (from ~24 in late 2023), with reported
  ~80–90% Phase I success vs ~52% historical — **but zero AI-discovered drugs have full
  regulatory approval yet**. [review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12472608/)

## State of research

**Best-performing now:** AI is **proven** at the discovery/design stage (structure prediction,
generative molecules, target ID) — and the first molecules are now producing real clinical
signals (Insilico's Phase 2a).

**Promising but unproven:** that AI-designed drugs **succeed in late-stage trials and reach
approval** — the actual bar that matters. No approvals exist yet.

**Open problems & weaknesses:** the high early-phase success rates likely reflect **selection/
survivorship bias** (only the most promising candidates advance) and small sample sizes;
**late-stage attrition** is the real open question. Timeline slips like Isomorphic's show how
hard the lab-to-clinic gap remains. This is the clinical extension of
[biology & proteins](biology-and-proteins.md).
