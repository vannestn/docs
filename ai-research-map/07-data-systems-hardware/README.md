## The shape of this area

Four through-lines: (1) **data quality is the quiet driver of capability** — classifier-
based curation and synthetic/recycled data deliver large gains and route around the "data
wall"; (2) **precision keeps dropping** — FP4 pretraining at trillion-token scale is the
biggest systems shift of the year; (3) **compute is decentralizing twice over** — across
datacenters (DiLoCo) and away from NVIDIA (TPU, Trainium, custom ASICs), with **HBM4 as the
universal chokepoint**; (4) **power, not chips, is now the binding constraint** — hyper-
scalers are buying nuclear baseload to keep scaling. The geopolitics of this (export
controls, sovereign compute) is in [12 · Geopolitics](../12-politics-capital-and-business-models/geopolitics-and-policy.md).
